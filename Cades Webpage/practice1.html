My Introduction to Programming

My coding journey began when a few social media ads prompted me to explore free learning platforms. Programs like FreeCodeCamp and other mobile coding apps provided my introduction to basic Python, and my interest quickly snowballed from there. Continuous research on Reddit, community forums, Claude, and ChatGPT helped bridge my knowledge gaps. This eventually led me to transition to a Linux Mint Cinnamon environment and adopt Visual Studio Code. Currently, I am focused on building practice projects to deepen my understanding and establish strong development habits.
A little about me.

I was born and raised in the US. I grew up in a small town in Kentucky, where tech is often overlooked and seemingly overrated to the folks in my town. As a 22-year-old father, balancing learning, studying, and practice presents unique challenges. Despite these obstacles, I dedicate time to my studies and frequently watch online courses during work breaks.
