The salon

A Mayfield salon, nine chairs deep

Salon Elite sits at 339 Charles Drive in Mayfield. Nine stylists rent booths here. Each one runs their own chair, sets their own prices and keeps their own schedule.

That is the useful part for you: you are not handed whoever happens to be free. You pick the stylist you want and book with them, and they are the person who does your hair each time.

Appointments are recommended — call ahead where you can, especially for colour and occasion styling, which need longer in the chair.

Questions

Before you call

How do I book an appointment?

Call (270) 804‑7272 and ask for the stylist you want by name, or message the salon on Facebook. Each stylist books their own clients, so you are arranging the time with them.

Can I ask for a particular stylist?

Yes — that is how the salon works. All nine are listed above. If you have not been in before and have no preference, say what you are after and we will point you to someone with space.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the stylist, your hair and how long the work takes, so there is no single price list. Each stylist sets their own prices — ask when you book and you will get a figure before any work starts.

What are your opening hours?

The salon is open Monday to Saturday and closed on Sunday, but each stylist keeps their own schedule, so hours differ from chair to chair. Ask your stylist for theirs, or check Facebook.

Do I need an appointment?

It is much safer to call ahead, especially for colour or occasion styling, which need longer in the chair. Whether a stylist can take a walk-in depends on how their day is booked.
