Privacy policy

Last updated 22 September 2026.

This policy explains what Salon Elite does with personal information in connection with this website. It covers the website only — what your stylist keeps about you as their client is separate, and they will explain that at your appointment.

The short version

  • This website has no contact form and collects nothing you type.
  • It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site, so no third party sees your visit.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

What is collected

Nothing, by us, through this site. There is no form to fill in and nothing is stored in your browser. Booking happens by phone or through our Facebook page, not through this website.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs — IP address, browser, and the page requested — automatically, for security and fault-finding. They are typically kept for under 30 days and we do not use them to build a profile of you.

If you call or message us

When you call the salon or message us on Facebook to book, your stylist keeps the details needed to make and keep the appointment. Anything you send through Facebook is also handled under Facebook’s own privacy policy, not this one.

Please do not send health information — allergies, medication, skin or scalp conditions — by message. Tell your stylist directly, where it can be recorded properly.

Cookies and tracking

As built, this website stores nothing on your device and sets no cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

If analytics are added later, a consent banner will appear first and no analytics cookie will be set unless you press Accept. Your choice would be remembered in your browser’s local storage under the key se-consent, which is used for nothing else and never leaves your device.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Several states, including Kentucky under the Kentucky Consumer Data Privacy Act, give residents statutory rights along these lines.

We honour these requests from anyone who asks, whether or not the law technically applies to a salon our size. Call (270) 804‑7272 or ask at the salon, and we will deal with it within 45 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone. Children are welcome in the salon, but a parent or guardian should make the booking.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. In particular, if a contact form or analytics are ever added, this page will be rewritten before they go live.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your information: call (270) 804‑7272, message us on Facebook, or call in at 339 Charles Dr, Mayfield, KY 42066.
