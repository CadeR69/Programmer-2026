Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our coffee company collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit, use our services, or make a purchase from our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you, automatically through your device, and from third parties.

  • Information you provide directly: Contact details (name, billing address, shipping address, email address, phone number), payment information, and account credentials.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, device type, browser information, time zone, and cookies.
  • Subscription Information: Details required to manage your recurring coffee shipments.

2. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use your personal information to fulfill orders, manage your coffee subscription account, process payments, and communicate updates to you.

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We share your personal information with trusted service providers to help us run our business (such as e-commerce platforms, payment processors, and shipping carriers).

4. Your Rights and Choices

You may have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal data. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

5. Contact Us

For questions or complaints regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at support@yourcoffeeshop.com.
