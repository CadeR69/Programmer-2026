2. Booking an appointment

Stylists at Salon Elite rent their own booths and book their own clients, so your appointment is an arrangement between you and your stylist rather than with the salon.

Book by calling (270) 804‑7272 and asking for your stylist by name, or by messaging us on Facebook. This website has no booking form. A message is a request until your stylist has confirmed a time with you.

3. Prices

No price list is published here, because each stylist sets their own prices and the figure depends on your hair and how long the work takes. Ask when you book and your stylist will give you a price before any work begins. Payment is due at the end of the appointment, to your stylist.

4. Deposits, cancellations and lateness

Some services may require a deposit, and we ask for reasonable notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment so the time can be offered to someone else. Arriving late may mean a shortened service or rebooking, since the appointment after yours cannot absorb the overrun.

Please ask us for the current deposit and cancellation terms when you book — they are confirmed with you at that point.

5. Patch tests and your safety

Colour services may require a skin patch test in advance. Your stylist will tell you whether one is needed and how far ahead it has to be done. This is a safety requirement, not a formality.

Please tell us before treatment about allergies, sensitivities, skin or scalp conditions, medication, recent treatments elsewhere, and pregnancy. We rely on what you tell us, and we cannot accept responsibility for a reaction caused by information we were not given.

6. Results

Hair responds differently from person to person. Your stylist will tell you honestly what is achievable, and will not carry out work they believe will damage your hair. We cannot guarantee an exact match to a reference photograph.

If you are unhappy with a service, tell us as soon as you can and we will do what we reasonably can to put right anything that falls short of what was agreed.

Salon Elite provides the premises. The stylists who work here rent their booths and operate independently: each one sets their own prices, hours and booking, and is responsible for the services they carry out. Where these terms say “we”, they mean whoever you are dealing with in that context.
