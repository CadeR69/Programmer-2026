This Privacy Policy explains what information Mayfield Printing collects through this website, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

When you submit our Contact form or Request a Quote form, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, business name, email address, phone number, and details about your printing project.

We do not collect payment information through this website, and we do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit only to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, or discuss your printing project with you. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Protect Your Information

This website uses HTTPS encryption to protect information submitted through our forms in transit. Our forms include spam-prevention measures, and submissions are rate-limited to help prevent abuse. Form submissions are processed through our website hosting provider and are only accessible to Mayfield Printing staff.

Data Retention

We retain the information you submit only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or complete your project, unless you ask us to remove it sooner.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update or delete information you have submitted at any time by contacting us using the information below.
